---
title: Materials & Mechanisms
summary: Understanding structure-property relationships and underlying
  mechanisms governing (photoresponsive) mixed conduction to design materials
  with tailored properties.
order: 2
---
Through employing our novel characterization techniques, we aim to develop a mechanistic framework describing photon-ion-electron coupling in organic mixed ionic-electronic conductors. Systematically varying materials is crucial to formulate design rules that allow to control functional material response.
