Pushing the boundaries of functional materials requires understanding their underlying mechanisms. Yet, to measure is not to know. True understanding emerges from well-designed experiments combined with theoretical insights, often drawing on multiple disciplines.



Tom van der Pol started as an Assistant Professor at Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e) on October 1, 2026. He obtained his BSc and MSc in Chemical Engineering and Chemistry at TU/e, including an internship at IBM Almaden, followed by a PhD investigating the optoelectronic properties of organic and perovskite semiconductors and photovoltaic devices. He subsequently joined Linköping University as a postdoctoral scholar and later as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow (2023-2026), including a research secondment at the University of Bern. During this period, his research increasingly focused on operando characterization and the coupled electronic and ionic processes governing organic mixed conductors.
