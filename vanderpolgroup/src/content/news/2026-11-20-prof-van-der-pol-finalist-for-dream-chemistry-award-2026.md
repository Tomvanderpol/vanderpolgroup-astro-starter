Based on his CV and the description of his "Dream-project" in chemistry, Tom van der Pol has been named as finalist for the Dream Chemistry Award 2026 ([https://www.dreamchemistryaward.org/results](https://www.dreamchemistryaward.org/results)). Tom finds it an enormous honour to be among the select few finalist for this years award, and is looking forward to connecting with these great chemists in Warsaw.
